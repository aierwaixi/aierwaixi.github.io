* I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Sydney, supervised by Prof. [Luping Zhou](https://sites.google.com/view/lupingzhou). Before that, I received my M.S. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Machine Learning & Data Science) from the University of California, San Diego, and my B.E. degree in Artificial Intelligence from Southeast University, advised by Prof. [Beilun Wang](https://xgbxscwx.seu.edu.cn/wang-labsite/).
* My research interests include computer vision, multi-modal learning and medical image analysis.
